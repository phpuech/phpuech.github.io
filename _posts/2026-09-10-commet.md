COMMET is an international multidisciplinary symposium bringing together researchers, clinicians, engineers, industry representatives and students working at the frontier of biomedical innovation. 

The symposium explores how cellular interactions with the physical and biochemical environment can inspire advanced biological models, diagnostic technologies and emerging biomedical solutions.

Aside, COMMET marks the tenth anniversary of LANSBIODYT-UNAM, celebrating a decade of scientific collaboration, technology development and biomimetic innovation.

Inscriptions, program and more here : https://commet.fciencias.unam.mx/
